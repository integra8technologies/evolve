01 / Engineering

Full-Stack Software Engineer Intern

Grow across the parts of a product, from user-facing features to the systems behind them.

What you could work on

  • Building and improving web application features across frontend and backend.
  • Working with APIs, databases, and the flow of data through a product.
  • Writing readable code, testing changes, and taking part in reviews.

What will help you thrive

  • Curiosity about software across the stack and a willingness to learn.
  • Some experience with programming, web development, or personal projects.
  • Clear communication, teamwork, and follow-through.
02 / Finance

Accountant Intern

Support accurate, organized financial operations while building practical accounting experience.

What you could work on

  • Organizing invoices, receipts, and supporting financial records.
  • Assisting with transaction entry, account reconciliations, and expense tracking.
  • Preparing spreadsheet summaries and helping maintain clear documentation.

What will help you thrive

  • Currently studying accounting, finance, or a related subject.
  • Careful attention to detail and familiarity with spreadsheets.
  • Integrity, discretion, and a willingness to learn.
