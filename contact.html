Confidentiality and fit

Start with only what you are ready to share.

A first conversation can stay at the level of goals and constraints. If sensitive details are needed, discuss appropriate confidentiality terms before sharing them.

Do I need to send a detailed brief?

No. A short description of the situation and what you hope to change is a useful place to start.

Can we discuss confidentiality before sharing details?

Yes. If the conversation requires sensitive information, raise that before sharing it so the right arrangements can be discussed.

What if we are not sure which service we need?

That is fine. We can use the initial conversation to understand the challenge and identify what kind of expertise or next step may be appropriate.
